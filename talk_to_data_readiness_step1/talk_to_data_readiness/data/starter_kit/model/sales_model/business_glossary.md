# Sales Business Glossary

## Pipeline

- **Total Pipeline:** Sum of the potential amount of all sales opportunities.
- **Weighted Pipeline:** Sum of opportunity forecast amounts, calculated using opportunity probability.
- **Open Opportunity:** An opportunity that has not been marked Won or Lost.
- **Open Deals:** Count of distinct open opportunities.

## Revenue and Performance

- **Won Revenue:** Total amount of opportunities with the Won stage.
- **Win Rate:** Won opportunity value divided by total pipeline value.
- **Average Deal Size:** Average potential amount across opportunities.

## Sales Activities

- **Activity:** A recorded sales interaction associated with an opportunity or sales representative.
- **Activity Count:** Total number of recorded sales activities.

## Business Rules

- Won and Lost opportunities are closed opportunities.
- Qualified, Proposal, and Negotiation opportunities are open opportunities.
- Weighted Pipeline is not the same as Total Pipeline.
- Won Revenue must include only Won opportunities.
- An opportunity can have multiple associated sales activities.
- Opportunity amount and forecast amount represent different business concepts.
