# HR Business Glossary

## Workforce

- **Total Employees:** Number of distinct employees in the workforce.
- **Exited Employees:** Number of distinct employees who have left the organization.
- **Attrition Rate:** Exited employees divided by the total employee population, according to the organization's approved HR reporting definition.

## Attendance

- **Attendance Hours:** Total recorded working hours.
- **Overtime Hours:** Total hours worked beyond regular working hours.
- **Average Working Hours:** Average recorded working hours per attendance record.
- **Absent Count:** Number of attendance records marked Absent.

## Recruitment

- **Time to Hire:** Number of days between application and hiring.
- **Average Time to Hire:** Average number of days taken to hire candidates.
- **Candidate Status:** Current recruitment stage of a candidate.

## Sensitive Information

- Employee salary is confidential compensation information.
- Employee email is personally identifiable contact information.
- Access to sensitive employee attributes must follow organizational permissions and data governance policies.

## Business Rules

- Attendance records are not equivalent to distinct employees.
- One employee can have multiple attendance records.
- Recruitment candidates and employees are different populations.
- Attrition calculations must use an approved definition and reporting period.
- Salary and email should not be exposed to unauthorized natural-language queries.
