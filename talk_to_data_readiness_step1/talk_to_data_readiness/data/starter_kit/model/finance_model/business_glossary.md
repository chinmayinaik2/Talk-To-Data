# Finance Business Glossary

## Revenue and Sales

- **Gross Sales:** Invoiced value of all order lines, excluding cancelled orders.
- **Net Revenue:** Invoiced value of completed orders only. Excludes cancelled and returned orders. This is the official Finance revenue figure.
- **Discount:** Difference between list price and invoiced value, excluding cancelled orders.

## Profitability

- **Margin:** Official Finance margin, calculated as Net Revenue minus the cost of completed lines.
- **Margin %:** Official Finance margin divided by Net Revenue.
- **Sales-Team Margin:** Gross Sales minus the cost of non-cancelled lines. This is an informal sales-team metric and is not the official Finance margin.

## Customers and Orders

- **Order:** One transaction identified by TRX_ID. An order can contain multiple lines.
- **Average Order Value:** Net Revenue divided by the number of completed orders.
- **Active Customer:** A customer with at least one non-cancelled order during the reporting period.
- **Online Order:** An order line where the online flag is set.

## Returns

- **Return Rate:** Returned line value divided by Gross Sales.

## Important Business Rules

- Net Revenue is the official Finance revenue measure.
- Gross Sales and Net Revenue are not interchangeable.
- Official Finance Margin and Sales-Team Margin are different metrics.
- A transaction may contain multiple order lines.
- Cancelled and returned transactions must be handled according to the definition of each metric.
